Short Information – Indian Coast Guard CGEPT 01/2027 & 02/2027 2026

Indian Coast Guard (Ministry of Defence) has released Indian Coast Guard CGEPT 01/2027 & 02/2027 Notification 2026 for 365 Posts of Navik (General Duty) 260 Posts, Navik (Domestic Branch) 25 Posts, Yantrik (Mechanical 28, Electrical 23, Electronics 09 = 60 Posts) and Hygienist-I 20 Posts. 10th / 12th / Diploma pass candidates can apply online from 06 October 2026 (11:00 AM) to 21 October 2026 (05:30 PM) at cgept.parikshasaarthi.in.
