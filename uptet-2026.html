UP Teacher Eligibility Test UPTET 2026

UPTET 2026 Advt No 01/UPTET/2026 : Short Details

Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission (UPESSC), Prayagraj has released the notification for Uttar Pradesh Teacher Eligibility Test UPTET 2026 for Primary Level I to V & Junior Level VI to VIII. All eligible candidates can apply online from 27 March 2026 to 03 May 2026. Read the full notification for eligibility, exam pattern, syllabus & languages.

UPTET 2026 Primary I to V & Junior VI to VIII Eligibility

UPTET Primary Level I-V Eligibility

  • Bachelor Degree in Any Stream with Passed / Appeared DELED (Final Year) Exam OR
  • Bachelor Degree in Any Stream with 50% Marks / Master Degree in Any Subject with B.Ed Degree (Passed / Appearing) OR
  • Bachelor Degree in Any Stream with 45% Marks / Master Degree in Any Subject with B.Ed Degree (Passed / Appearing) (As per NCTE Norms) OR
  • 10+2 Intermediate 50% Marks with 4 Year B.ElEd OR
  • More Details Read Notification.

UPTET Junior Level VI-VIII Eligibility

  • Bachelor Degree / Master Degree with 50% Marks with B.Ed / B.Ed Special Exam Passed / Appearing (Final Year) OR
  • Bachelor Degree / Master Degree with BTC Training 2 Year Exam Passed OR
  • 10+2 Intermediate Exam with 50% Marks and B.ElEd 4 Year Course OR
  • 10+2 Inter Exam with 50% Marks and B.Sc Ed Exam Passed OR
  • Bachelor Degree in Any Stream with 45% Marks / Master Degree in Any Subject with B.Ed Degree (Passed / Appearing) (As per NCTE Norms) OR
