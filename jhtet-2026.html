Jharkhand Teacher Eligibility Test JHTET 2026

JAC TET 2026 Exam : Short Details of Notification

Jharkhand Academic Council, Ranchi has released the notification for Jharkhand Teacher Eligibility Test (JHTET 2026) for Primary Level Class I to V & Junior Level Class VI to VIII. All eligible candidates can apply online from 21 April 2026 to 20 July 2026. Read the full notification for eligibility, exam pattern & syllabus.

  • Application Begin : 21/04/2026
  • Last Date for Registration : 20/07/2026
  • Pay Exam Fee Last Date : 20/07/2026
  • JAC JHTET Exam Date : As per Schedule
  • Admit Card Available : Before Exam
  • Answer Key Available : After Exam
  • Result Declared : Notified Soon

Application Fee

  • For Paper I or Paper II Only :
  • General / BC / EWS : ₹1300/-
  • SC / ST : ₹700/-
  • For Both Paper (Junior / Primary) :
  • General / BC / EWS : ₹1500/-

Jharkhand JHTET 2026 Exam : Eligibility Details

Primary Level Class I-V Eligibility

  • 10+2 Intermediate Senior Secondary (or its equivalent) with at least 50% marks and passed or appearing in final year of 2-year Diploma in Elementary Education (B.T.C./D.El.Ed.) OR
  • 10+2 Intermediate Senior Secondary (or its equivalent) with at least 45% marks and passed or appearing in final year of 2-year Diploma in Elementary Education (B.T.C./D.El.Ed.), in accordance with the NCTE (Recognition Norms and Procedure), Regulations, 2002 OR
  • 10+2 Intermediate Senior Secondary (or its equivalent) with at least 50% marks and passed or appearing in final year of 4-year Bachelor of Elementary Education (B.El.Ed.) OR
  • 10+2 Intermediate Senior Secondary (or its equivalent) with at least 50% marks and passed or appearing in final year of 2-year Diploma in Education (Special Education) OR
  • Bachelor Degree (or its equivalent) and passed or appearing in final year of two year Diploma in Elementary Education (B.T.C./D.El.Ed.) OR
  • More Eligibility Details Read the Notification

Junior Level Class VI-VIII Eligibility

  • Bachelor Degree (or its equivalent) and passed or appearing in final year of 2-Year Diploma in Elementary Education (B.T.C./D.El.Ed.) OR
  • Bachelor Degree (or its equivalent) with at least 50% marks and passed or appearing in final year of Bachelor in Education (B.Ed/L.T./Shiksha Shastri (Regular)) OR
  • Bachelor Degree (or its equivalent) with at least 45% marks and passed or appearing in final year of Bachelor in Education (B.Ed/L.T./Shiksha Shastri (Regular)), in accordance with the NCTE Regulations OR
  • 10+2 Intermediate / Senior Secondary (or its equivalent) with at least 50% marks and passed or appearing in final year of 4-year Bachelor of Elementary Education (B.El.Ed) OR
  • 10+2 Intermediate / Senior Secondary (or its equivalent) with at least 50% marks and passed or appearing in final year of 4-year B.A/B.Sc.Ed or B.A.Ed/B.Sc.Ed OR
  • Bachelor Degree (or its equivalent) with at least 50% marks and passed or appearing in final year of B.Ed. (Special Education) OR
  • Master Degree with 55% Marks and 3 Year Integrated B.Ed-M.Ed Course OR
