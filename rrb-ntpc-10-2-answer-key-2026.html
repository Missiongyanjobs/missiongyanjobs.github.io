How To Check & Download RRB NTPC UG Answer Key 2026
  1. Visit the official website of your regional RRB or https://www.rrbapply.gov.in
  2. On homepage, click on “CEN (NTPC) Under Graduate Level Answer Key 2026”
  3. You will be redirected to login page
  4. Enter your Registration Number, Date of Birth and Captcha Code
  5. Click on Login / Submit button
  6. Your Answer Key and Response Sheet will appear on screen
  7. Download PDF and match your responses
  8. Take a printout for future reference
