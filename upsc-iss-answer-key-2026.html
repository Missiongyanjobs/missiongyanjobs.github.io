How To Check & Download UPSC IES/ISS Answer Key 2026
  1. Go to the official website of UPSC: https://www.upsc.gov.in
  2. On homepage, find and open “Examination” section
  3. Click on “Answer Keys” or “Active Examinations”
  4. Look for “IES/ISS Examination 2026” link
  5. Click on “Statistics-I” and “Statistics-II” Answer Key PDF
  6. Download the provisional answer key PDF
  7. Match your responses and calculate marks
  8. Save or print PDF for future reference
UPSC IES/ISS 2026 : Mode Of Selection
  • Stage 1: Written Examination - 1000 Marks. 6 Papers for both IES & ISS
  • Stage 2: Interview/Personality Test - 200 Marks
  • Final Merit: Based on Written Exam + Interview marks
  • Note: Statistics I & II papers are common for ISS. Economics papers for IES.
