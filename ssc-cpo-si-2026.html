Short Information – SSC CPO SI 2026

Staff Selection Commission (SSC) has released SSC CPO SI CAPF Notification 2026 for 1871 Sub-Inspector Posts. This includes Delhi Police SI Male 205 + Female 112 = 317 Posts, CAPF GD SI Male 1173 + Female 147 = 1320 Posts (CRPF, BSF, CISF, ITBP, SSB) and CISF Fire SI Male 234 Posts. Graduation pass male/female candidates can apply online from 10 September 2026 to 30 September 2026 (Till 11:00 PM) at official website ssc.gov.in. Read full details below.
