How To Check & Download SSC CGL Tier-II Final Answer Key 2026
  1. Go to the official website of SSC: https://ssc.gov.in
  2. Login using your Registration Number and Password
  3. Click on “Answer Key” section on dashboard
  4. Select “Combined Graduate Level Examination 2025 - Tier-II”
  5. Click on “Final Answer Key & Response Sheet” link
  6. Download PDF and match your responses with final answer key
  7. Check your marks using response sheet
  8. Take a printout for future reference
SSC CGL 2025 : Mode Of Selection
  • Tier-I Exam: Computer Based Examination - Qualifying in nature
  • Tier-II Exam: Computer Based Examination - Paper I Compulsory for all, Paper II for JSO/Statistical Investigator
  • Document Verification: For shortlisted candidates after final result
  • Final Merit: Based on Tier-II marks only. Tier-I marks not counted for final selection.
